Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026
Summary: BlanketCozzy is built with a zero-knowledge architecture. We do not collect, log, track, sell, or transmit any personal data, usage habits, keystrokes, or network details to any external server. All communication happens strictly inside your local Wi-Fi network.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the BlanketCozzy - Trackpad mobile application (the "App") and the BlanketCozzy Desktop Companion (the "Companion Software"). BlanketCozzy is designed to function as a wireless trackpad, keyboard, and presentation remote controller between your mobile device and your personal computer over a local area network (LAN).

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

BlanketCozzy does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically:

  • No Account Required: You do not need an email, phone number, password, or account to use the application.
  • No Keystroke Logging: Any text sent through the Remote Typing feature is converted directly into keyboard input events on your local machine and is never cached, logged, or recorded.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: We do not embed Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, Crashlytics, or any third-party behavioral analytics SDKs.
  • No Advertising: There are no advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or cross-app tracking identifiers in the App.
  • No Cloud Servers: Touch gestures and commands are never uploaded to any cloud server or third-party service.

3. Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

To perform its core functions as a remote input device, the App requests only standard Android permissions:

  • INTERNET: Required to establish a direct local TCP socket connection between your phone and your computer's IP address. No internet connectivity outside of your local router is accessed.
  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATE: Used solely to detect whether your device is connected to a local Wi-Fi network and calculate local subnet parameters for device discovery.
  • CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE: Enables the phone to listen for UDP discovery beacons (on port 8766) sent by your computer to automatically detect your Mac or PC without requiring manual IP entry.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide tactile micro-haptic feedback when tapping or swiping on the trackpad.

4. Local Network Communication

When you use BlanketCozzy:

  • The companion desktop server broadcasts a small announcement beacon containing its hostname and local IP address within your private Wi-Fi subnet.
  • When connected, raw touchpad coordinates and click commands are sent directly to your computer's local port (default 8765) using low-latency TCP.
  • This data never leaves your local Wi-Fi router. If you disconnect your router from the global Internet, BlanketCozzy will continue to work normally offline.

5. Children's Privacy

Because the App collects zero personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. The App is designed in a manner consistent with COPPA and GDPR requirements, though we recommend reviewing applicable local regulations before deployment in regulated environments.

6. Security of Your Data

Because BlanketCozzy - Trackpad does not use a cloud relay or store your trackpad traffic on external servers, your control data is designed to remain entirely within your local network. No personal data is held on our infrastructure to lose, sell, or breach.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
